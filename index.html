TRANS
FICT
🪪
  1. Truck arrives at the yard gate
  2. Guard asks for the driver ID and papers
  3. ID, booking and container seal are checked
  4. Approved: the barrier opens
  5. Truck drives to the lifting bay
  6. Guard clears the area and gives the operator the go-ahead
  7. Operator confirms and lowers the spreader
  8. Twist-locks engaged and confirmed
  9. Slow lift with nobody under the load
  10. Controlled move across the yard
  11. Gentle placement on level ground or truck bed
  12. Twist-locks released, container logged
  13. Truck leaves and the gate closes
