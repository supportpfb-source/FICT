Who we are

Fatima InterOcean Container Terminal (FICT) is a container-yard and logistics-focused business built around dependable container storage, yard handling and warehouse support.

We give our customers an organised place to receive, store, move and manage shipping containers, for both national and international business. Our office team coordinates customer requests, yard records and movement planning, while our yard staff work to keep workflows clear and organised.
